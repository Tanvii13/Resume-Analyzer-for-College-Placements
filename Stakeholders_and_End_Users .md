## (a) Identification of Stakeholders and End Users

### Stakeholders / End Users

1. **Students (End Users)**
   - Primary users who upload resumes for analysis and placement preparation.
   - Require **resume scoring, skill analysis, job/internship matching, and improvement suggestions**.

2. **Placement Cell / Placement Officers**
   - Responsible for supporting students during internship and placement processes.
   - Need **student resume insights, common issue identification, and placement-readiness support**.

3. **Faculty / Placement Coordinators**
   - Guide students in preparing resumes and improving their profiles.
   - Need **clear feedback, skill relevance analysis, and recommendations** to help students improve.

4. **Recruiters / HR Professionals**
   - Review candidate profiles and are interested in relevant skills and suitable candidates.
   - Need **relevant skill information, resume quality, and effective candidate-job matching**.

5. **System Administrators**
   - Manage and maintain the application and its data.
   - Responsible for **user management, data security, system maintenance, and application availability**.

6. **College / University Management**
   - Interested in improving students' placement readiness and placement support.
   - Need **overall insights into student preparation and resume quality**.

7. **Job / Internship Data Providers**
   - Provide information about available jobs, internships, roles, and required skills.
   - Require **accurate and updated job/internship information** for effective matching.

8. **Development Team**
   - Responsible for designing, developing, testing, and maintaining the system.
   - Require **stakeholder feedback, clear requirements, and system performance information**.
