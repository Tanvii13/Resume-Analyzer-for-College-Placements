Privacy rules

  • Students agree to the privacy notice before their first upload.
  • Files are stored privately under random names, never at a public address. No one can download another person's file.
  • Staff see the analysis only, not the phone number, links or full resume text.
  • Every time staff open a student's analysis it's logged, and the student sees it on their dashboard. Logs hold names and file names, never resume text.
  • Students can download everything stored about them and delete any resume.
  • Uploads older than {{ retention_days }} days are deleted automatically (checked every hour).
