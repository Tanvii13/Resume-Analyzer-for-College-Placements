Privacy

What we store, why, who can see it, how long it's kept, and how results are worked out.

What we store, and why

  • Your account: name, college email, role, and your password as a salted hash (never the password itself).
  • Your resumes: the file, its text, and the analysis made from it.
  • Logs: when placement staff open your analysis, and changes admins make. Logs never hold resume text.
  • It's used only to help you prepare for placements and to help the placement cell support students. It isn't sold, shared outside the college, or used to train AI models.

Who can see it

  • You: everything about your own account.
  • Placement officers and admins: your analysis (score, checks, skills, sections found), not your phone number, links or full resume text. Each time they open it, it's recorded and you can see it on your dashboard.
  • Admins also manage accounts and roles. Nobody else can see your data, and files are never at a public address.

How long it's kept

Uploads are deleted automatically {{ retention_days }} days after you upload them. You can delete any resume yourself from its page, and download everything stored about you from your dashboard.

How results are worked out

Results are supporting information. The tool never accepts, rejects or selects anyone: placement decisions are made by people.
